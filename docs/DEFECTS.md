# Good vs defective (definition for M5)

A part is **GOOD** when the body has both sensor holes clear and undamaged, no cracks or broken corners,
and (if assembled) the lid is fully seated.

| # | Defect | How I make the sample (repeatable, no reprint needed) | Visible cue |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | **Blocked hole** | small piece of tape / paper / blu-tack over one hole | one hole not round/open |
| D2 | **Broken / chipped corner** | the older print, or snip a corner with cutters on a spare | missing corner on the outline |
| D3 | **Lid missing / not seated** | body without lid, or lid pushed half-way | lid box absent or offset |
| D4 | **Surface defect** | marker stain / stringing / tape strip across the face | dark mark on the face |

Pass/fail test set (M5.2): **10 good + 10 defective** (≥ 2 of each defect type), shot on the fixed rig.
A **false pass** (defective classified GOOD) is the critical error; target ≤ 1 of 10.
