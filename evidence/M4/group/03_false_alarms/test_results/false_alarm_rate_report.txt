2. THRESHOLD TUNING RATIONALE:
   - Challenge Identified: In initial testing, normal rotational acceleration
     produced sudden current inrush pulses (~0.065A-0.146A) and acoustic clicks.
     Because initial static training data had near-zero variance (std ~ 0.001),
     normal motion produced massive Z-scores (Z > 300), triggering false alarms.
   - Engineering Solution 1: Incorporated physical standard deviation floors
     (FEATURE_STD_FLOORS) in raspberry_pi/models/anomaly_detector.py.
   - Engineering Solution 2: Calibrated normal operational current limit to 0.200A
     (200mA) in config.py. Free rotation pulses (~0.146A) safely clear this
     boundary while mechanical binding (>0.220A) triggers immediate detection.
